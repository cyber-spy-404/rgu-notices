Direct Broadcast to 1,000+ Students via Google Groups

When new notices are found, the engine sends 1 single email to your Google Group. Google distributes the alert and all downloadable circulars to every enrolled member with zero Gmail rate limits or daily throttling.

How Google Groups Broadcast Works
1
Create Google Group Visit groups.google.com → "Create group" (e.g. rgu-notices@googlegroups.com).
2
Add Students Invite or directly add your 1,000+ student email addresses to the group.
3
Set Posting Permissions In Group Settings → Posting permissions, authorize your Dispatcher Gmail address to post messages.
4
Save & Verify Save your Google Group address and App Password, then click "Send Test Notice to Group"!
